Deepwork Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Deepwork: Focus & Tasks ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect your data

The app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no servers, and we cannot see your projects, tasks or focus sessions.

Where your data is stored

Version 1.0: your projects, tasks, focus sessions and settings are stored only on your device.

Version 1.1 and later: your projects, tasks, subtasks and focus sessions are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Deepwork, in your own private iCloud database so they stay the same on your devices. This database belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot read it. It is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and counts toward your iCloud storage. Timer settings stay on each device. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Deepwork in the Settings app, nothing new is sent to iCloud.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device for focus sessions and for tasks that are due. These are created and delivered by your device only.

Apple Calendar (optional)

If you switch on "Add to Apple Calendar", the app asks for access to your calendars. It creates its own "Deepwork" calendar and adds your dated tasks and projects to it. It does not read, change or share your other calendars or events. If your calendars sync with iCloud or another account, that sync is handled by Apple or that provider under their own privacy policy. Switching the option off removes the Deepwork calendar.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project or task in the app. With iCloud sync, deleting it on one device deletes it on your other devices too.

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. With iCloud sync, a copy stays in your iCloud so it can return if you reinstall the app. To delete that copy, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then your iCloud storage, choose Deepwork and delete its data. This removes it from iCloud.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
