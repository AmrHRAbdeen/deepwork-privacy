Deepwork Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Deepwork: Focus & Tasks ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We don't collect your data

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your projects, tasks, focus sessions and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device for focus sessions and for tasks that are due. These are created and delivered by your device only.

Apple Calendar (optional)

If you switch on "Add to Apple Calendar", the app asks for access to your calendars. It creates its own "Deepwork" calendar and adds your dated tasks and projects to it. It does not read, change or share your other calendars or events. If your calendars sync with iCloud or another account, that sync is handled by Apple or that provider under their own privacy policy. Switching the option off removes the Deepwork calendar.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
